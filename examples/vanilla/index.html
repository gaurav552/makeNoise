    A player that stays out of the way

    Start a playlist from the left. The player appears as a small medallion in the corner and stays there while you read the rest of this page.

    What to try

    Move your pointer onto the medallion. After a moment it opens into a small card with the title, a seek bar and the controls. Move away and it folds back into the corner. Tab to it with the keyboard and it opens the same way. Escape closes it.

    The list icon in the card opens the queue. The queue stays open until you close it, so you can keep adding tracks from the playlists and watch them arrive. Drag the dotted handle to reorder, or focus a row and press Alt with the up and down arrows. Delete removes a row. None of this touches the playlists on the left.

    While you read

    After a few seconds without attention the medallion fades back a little, so it doesn't pull your eye while you read. When the track changes, its title shows next to the medallion for a moment and then disappears. Long titles wrap onto two lines in the card instead of being cut short.

    The ring around the artwork is the progress through the current track. Shuffle and repeat live in the card, next to previous and next. Repeat cycles through off, all and one.

    When something goes wrong

    The last playlist contains a file that doesn't exist. Play it and the player skips the missing track, then says which one it skipped. If every track in the queue fails, it stops and explains why instead of trying forever.

    Some browsers block audio until you interact with the page. If that happens, the card asks you to press play.

    Styling it

    Change the corner and the theme in the header. In your own app you can also set colors, fonts, size and offsets with CSS custom properties on the element, and style its inner parts with ::part().
